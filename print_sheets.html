TABLE REFERENCE
OUTCOME TRACK
MARGIN
+4 or more STRIKEOUT (K) Runners hold
0 to +3 ROUTINE OUT Flyout / Pop
-1 to -3 FIELDED / SAC Runner 3rd scores!
-4 to -5 WALK (BB) Forces runners
-6 to -8 CLEAN SINGLE Runners +1 (2nd 60%)
-9 or less EXTRA-BASE / HR d6: 1-4 2B, 5 3B, 6 HR
MARGIN: Pitcher Roll Minus Batter Roll. Ties keep token!
TOKEN & TRACKER
MOMENTUM / TTOP
RULES
MOMENTUM TOKEN:
• Winner of read claims token.
• TIE: Token does NOT move! Current holder keeps it!
• Holder rolls personal Momentum Die with their 2 stats.

PITCHER FATIGUE (TTOP):
When opposing #1 Leadoff steps in, move peg down:
• Loop 1: Full Momentum Die
• Loop 2: Step Down (d8 to d6, d6 to d4)
• Loop 3: Exhausted! Call Bullpen!
Relievers enter fresh with full base Momentum!
