⚾ FULL COUNT — PLAYMAT QUICK RULES REFERENCE

1. SIMULTANEOUS PITCH GUESS (🔴 / 🔵 / 🟢)
• Both managers secretly select and reveal one Color Stance card:
  — 🔴 Red: Pitcher VEL+GRT vs. Batter POW+CON
  — 🔵 Blue: Pitcher CMD+GRT vs. Batter EYE+FOC
  — 🟢 Green: Pitcher MOV+DEC vs. Batter CON+SPD
• 🎯 MATCH (Same Color): Batter claims the ⚡ Momentum Token!
• 🌀 MISMATCH (Different Colors): Pitcher claims the ⚡ Momentum Token!
2. ROLL DICE & EXTRA-BASE POW DIE
• Each player rolls the 2 Attribute Dice for the color they chose.
• The ⚡ Momentum Holder also rolls their current Momentum Die (d12, d8, or d6 where their cube sits).
• Margin = Pitcher Total − Batter Total.
• On an Extra-Base Hit (−7 or less), roll the Batter's POW Die: 1–5 = Double (2B), 6 = Triple (3B), 7+ = Home Run (HR)!
3. MOMENTUM CUBE TRACK & SPECIAL ABILITIES
• Place a cube on each player's START box (d12, d8, or d6).
• Tap any active card's ⚡ Special Ability box before pitching/swinging to arm it. Using an ability slides that player's cube 1 box right (d12 ▸ d8 ▸ d6 ▸ OUT ✕) after the at-bat resolves.
4. PITCHER ENDURANCE & SENDING RUNNERS
• Starters (SP): Slide cube 1 box right each time BATTER #1 starts a new loop through the batting order.
• Relievers (RP): Slide cube 1 box right at the start of each new inning they remain on the mound.
• Single with Runner on 2nd: Hold at 3rd (safe) OR Send Home (roll runner's SPD die: 4+ Safe / 1–3 Out).
