# Full Count: Working Deck V2 — Special Powers & Momentum Degradation

Welcome to the **V2 Working Deck** of *Full Count*. In this edition, every player on both rosters possesses a unique, game-changing superpower. However, superpowers are physically and mentally exhausting: **every activation permanently degrades the player's Momentum Die down the polyhedral ladder**.

---

## 1. The Momentum Degradation System

Every player begins the game with a printed **Starting Momentum Die** (`d8`, `d6`, or `d4`), which represents both their competitive momentum bonus and their superpower stamina:

```
[ d8 ] ──(Degrades)──> [ d6 ] ──(Degrades)──> [ d4 ] ──(Degrades)──> [ 🚫 EXHAUSTED / NONE ]
3 Charges              2 Charges              1 Charge               0 Charges Left
```

### The Rules of Degradation:
1. **Power Activation Cost**: Whenever a player activates their printed Special Power, you must immediately step their momentum die down one notch on the ladder.
   - A `d8` player can use their power up to **3 times** before burning out (`d8` ➔ `d6` ➔ `d4` ➔ `None`).
   - A `d6` player can use their power up to **2 times** (`d6` ➔ `d4` ➔ `None`).
   - A `d4` player can use their power **exactly 1 time** (`d4` ➔ `None`).
2. **Current Momentum Die in At-Bats**:
   - Whenever that player holds or wins the Momentum Token during an at-bat, they roll their **currently available** momentum die.
   - If they have used their power until they reach `🚫 Exhausted`, they **no longer receive a momentum die** when holding momentum (they roll only their 2 approach dice).
3. **No Power Without Fuel**: Once a player is `🚫 Exhausted` (no momentum die left), their Special Power can no longer be activated for the rest of the game.
4. **Physical Tracking on Tabletop**:
   - Players track degradation simply by rotating their polyhedral die on their player card to show the current die size (`d8` ➔ `d6` ➔ `d4`), or removing the die when burned out.
