LADDER & FATIGUE
RULES
MOMENTUM LADDER LEVELS:
• L3 🔥 (LOCKED IN): +5 Bonus & Superpower Unlocked!
• L2 (PRIME ★): +3 Bonus (Stars start here).
• L1 (READY): +2 Bonus (Role players start here).
• ✕ EXH: +0 Bonus (Abilities locked; cannot spend).

PITCHER FATIGUE (TIMES THROUGH ORDER):
• 2nd time facing #1 Leadoff: Slide cube 1 step down.
• 3rd time facing #1 Leadoff: Slide cube 1 step down.
• 3+ runs allowed in an inning: Slide cube 1 step down immediately!
SPENDING FUEL: Activating Special Ability drops cube 1 step left!
TABLEPLAY MASTER
BASERUNNING & QUICK REF
REF
SINGLES & SPEED DIE:
• Runners advance 1 base safely.
• Runner on 2nd may attempt to score: Roll SPD die (4+ Safe at Home, 1-3 Out at Plate!).

DOUBLES & TRIPLES:
• Double: Runners on 2nd and 3rd score; runner on 1st goes to 3rd.
• Triple: All runners on base score; batter stops at 3rd base.

WALKS: Only force-advance runners on base.
SURGES: When you trigger your card's ⚡ SURGE, slide cube 1 step right!
