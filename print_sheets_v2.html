V2 SPECIAL RULES
⚡ POWER SYSTEM
RULES
💥 ACTIVATION & DEGRADATION:
Every time a player uses their Special Power, their Momentum Die degrades down the polyhedral ladder:
[ d8 ] ➜ [ d6 ] ➜ [ d4 ] ➜ [ 🚫 NONE ]
• d8 Starters: 3 Uses before burning out.
• d6 Regulars: 2 Uses before burning out.
• d4 Specialists: 1 Use (One bullet only!).
• Holding Momentum: When you hold momentum, you roll your currently available die. If exhausted, you get NO bonus die!
PRO TIP: Save high-tier powers for bases-loaded or late 9th-inning high-leverage showdowns!

GAME MECHANICS
AT-BAT SUMMARY
GUIDE
1. Stance Selection: Pitcher and batter secretly choose 1 of 3 Stance Cards (🔴 Heat/Power, 🔵 Zone/Eye, 🟢 Break/Timing) and reveal simultaneously.
2. Momentum Read: Winner of rock-paper-scissors claims Momentum Token and rolls their currently available Momentum Die!
3. Roll Dice: Roll 2 approach dice + Momentum Die (if holding token).
4. Special Powers: May activate before, during, or after rolls as printed on card. Degrade Momentum 1 tier per use!
5. Calculate Margin: Higher total subtracts lower total and consults Table 1 or 2!
STAMINA CHECK: When your Momentum Die reaches 🚫 NONE, you cannot activate powers and get no momentum bonus die.
